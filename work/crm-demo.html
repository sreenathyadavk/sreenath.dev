The Objective

Beyond experimental AI systems and personal projects, real-world engineering often requires navigating massive, existing enterprise codebases. The CRM Demo project proves my ability to deploy, customize, and manage production-grade business software stacks designed for complex team workflows.

Implementation Approach

This project is a tailored deployment of Twenty, an open-source, enterprise-grade CRM architecture. Rather than reinventing the wheel, the engineering challenge here is DevOps and system integration—setting up the required PostgreSQL databases, Redis caching layers, and handling complex environment configurations to ensure a robust deployment.

Business Automation

By customizing and deploying a massive CRM stack, I am able to provide technical teams with the building blocks they need. The resulting architecture gives clients a custom CRM that meets complex business needs, integrating with automation pipelines and serving as a central data source for business intelligence.
